Understanding Our Private-Pay Model

Our private-pay model allows us to serve clients who might not meet strict insurance criteria, ensuring more individuals in the Black Hills can access quality speech therapy.

You’ll pay for services at the time of each session. After payment, we provide a superbill—a detailed invoice you can submit to your insurance for possible reimbursement. This model allows us to offer flexible care tailored to your needs, regardless of insurance restrictions.
