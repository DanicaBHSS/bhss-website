Explore Our Services

Child Therapy

Little voices, big wins!

Got a kiddo who’s ready to chat up a storm? Our child therapy sessions are all about turning those mumbles into magic! We bring the fun with play-based, evidence-backed activities that help your little one shine—whether they’re working on their first words or mastering a tricky “r” sound. We’ll meet them where they’re at, either at home or online, and cheer them on every step of the way. Let’s make talking a fun adventure!

  • Language Delay (getting those words flowing)
  • Lisp (turning “s” into a superstar sound!)
  • Autism Spectrum Disorder (building social skills with high-fives!)

  • Phonological Disorders (making tricky sounds a piece of cake!)

    …and the list goes on!

Adult Therapy

Rediscover your voice!

Hey grown-ups, we’ve got your back—whether you’re bouncing back after a stroke or tackling a speech challenge that’s been bugging you! Our adult therapy is all about giving you the tools to communicate with confidence again. We’ll bring the session to your cozy Black Hills home or hop online, keeping things upbeat and tailored just for you. Let’s get you chatting like a pro—your voice deserves a comeback!

  • Aphasia (finding words again after a stroke—let’s do this!)
  • Dysarthria (making speech clearer, one step at a time!)
  • Stuttering (smoothing out speech for confident chats!)

  • Voice Disorders (bringing back that smooth, strong voice!)

    ….and more!

Online Therapy

Speech at the kitchen table!

Can’t meet in person? No worries—we’ll bring the speech party to you with our online therapy! Our teletherapy sessions are secure, interactive, and oh-so-fun, perfect for kids and adults who love the convenience of chatting from home. We use a HIPAA-compliant platform with cool tools like screen-sharing and virtual games to keep things lively, whether you’re in Spearfish or a remote Black Hills spot!

  • Articulation Challenges (nailing those sounds via video—woo!)
  • Language Development (growing vocab from your couch!)
  • Fluency Issues (smoothing speech, screen-style!)
  • Social Communication (practicing chit-chat with digital high-fives!)

  • Rehabilitation Needs (recovering skills with virtual cheerleading!)

    …it doesn’t stop there.
